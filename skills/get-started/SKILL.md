# Get started with siliXon

1. Call `list_projects`. If it asks the person to sign in, tell them a siliXon account is needed.
2. Say in two or three sentences what they can do here: describe a board for Rocky to design, attach a sketch, photo or datasheet, import a KiCad project, or check a board for manufacturing.
3. If they have projects, offer their latest by name. Otherwise suggest one small first board, such as an LED that blinks from USB-C.

For help, or to talk with the team and other users, point them to siliXon's Discord: https://discord.gg/k9rjZxVpED.

Do not start a design until they say what they want. When they do, use the `design-a-board` skill.
